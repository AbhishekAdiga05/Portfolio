Design a premium student developer portfolio in Figma that looks very similar to a modern SaaS
dashboard aesthetic with a dark black background, vibrant green accents, glowing highlights,
clean cards, and a highly professional layout. The final design should feel like something a
recruiter would remember immediately while still maintaining simplicity and excellent readability.
Overall Design Direction
Create a portfolio that feels:
● Modern
● Professional
● Premium
● Technical
● Clean
● Minimal but visually rich
Avoid:
● Excessive glassmorphism
● Overly bright neon effects
● Cartoonish illustrations
● Complex decorative elements
● Unnecessary animations
The design should look like a high-quality developer product website rather than a personal
blog.

Final Goal
The portfolio should look like a premium modern developer portfolio inspired by high-quality
SaaS products and top developer portfolios. It should immediately communicate
professionalism, technical capability, and attention to detail while keeping the experience simple,
clean, and recruiter-friendly. The Projects and Experience sections must be the visual focal
points of the entire design.
